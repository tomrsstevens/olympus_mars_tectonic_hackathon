COMPANY A BELGIUM - VACATION POLICY
Document owner: HR Belgium (Pieter Janssens)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2025-12-18
Last reviewed: 2026-01-15

Legal vacation: employees with a full-time five-day week are entitled to 20 legal vacation days,
based on the days worked in the previous calendar year.

Company days: Company A Belgium adds 6 extra vacation days, for a total of 26 vacation days per year.

Requests: submit vacation requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of a week or more.
