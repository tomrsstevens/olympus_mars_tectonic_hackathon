COMPANY A BELGIUM - WORK RULES (ARBEIDSREGLEMENT): WORKING HOURS
Document owner: HR Belgium (Pieter Janssens)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2025-09-01
Last reviewed: 2025-11-20

Full-time employees work 38 hours per week, 7 hours 36 minutes per day, Monday to Friday.
Flexible start between 07:30 and 09:30; core hours are 09:30 to 15:30.
Overtime is only worked at the request of your manager and is compensated with recovery time.
