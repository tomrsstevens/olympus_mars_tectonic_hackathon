EMPLOYEE WORKING CONDITIONS POLICY
Company: Company A (Belgium)
Policy ID: HR-BE-004
Effective date: 1 January 2026
Version: 3.1
Policy owner: Human Resources Belgium
Status: Approved

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all permanent employees of Company A working under a Belgian employment contract, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The standard full-time working schedule consists of 38 hours per week, normally distributed across five working days from Monday to Friday. Alternative schedules may be agreed with the employee's manager, subject to applicable working-time rules. Any working time beyond the agreed schedule must receive prior approval. Approved overtime shall be compensated at 150% of the employee's standard hourly rate or, where agreed, through equivalent compensatory time off.

3. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to a minimum of 21 paid vacation days per calendar year. After each completed year of continuous employment, employees receive one additional paid vacation day, up to a maximum of 26 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR platform and are subject to manager approval.

4. Remote Work
Employees whose role is suitable for remote work may work remotely for up to 2 days per working week. Remote-working days must be coordinated with the employee's manager and may be restricted where physical presence is required.

5. Sick Leave
Employees unable to work due to illness must notify their manager and Human Resources as soon as reasonably possible. A medical certificate is required when an absence exceeds 2 consecutive working days.

6. Meal Vouchers
Eligible employees receive meal vouchers with a total value of €8 per working day. Meal vouchers are provided only for days on which the employee has effectively performed work.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Employees may request parental leave, maternity or paternity leave, bereavement leave and other forms of special leave in accordance with applicable Belgian employment legislation and internal HR procedures.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
