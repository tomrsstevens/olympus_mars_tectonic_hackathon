EMPLOYEE WORKING CONDITIONS POLICY
Company: Company A (Belgium)
Policy ID: HR-BE-004
Effective date: 1 January 2024
Version: 2.4
Policy owner: Human Resources Belgium
Status: Superseded

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all permanent employees of Company A working under a Belgian employment contract, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The standard full-time working schedule consists of 38 hours per week from Monday to Friday. Approved overtime shall be compensated at 150% of the employee's standard hourly rate or through equivalent compensatory time off.

3. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to a minimum of 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. After each completed year of continuous employment, employees receive one additional paid vacation day, up to a maximum of 25 paid vacation days per calendar year.

4. Remote Work
Employees whose role is suitable for remote work may work remotely for up to 3 days per working week, in agreement with their manager.

5. Sick Leave
Employees unable to work due to illness must notify their manager immediately. A medical certificate is required from the first day of absence.

6. Meal Vouchers
Eligible employees receive meal vouchers with a total value of €7 per working day.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Parental and special leave are available in accordance with applicable Belgian employment legislation.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
