COMPANY A BELGIUM - PAYROLL POLICY (LOONBELEID)
Document owner: Payroll Belgium (Ann Peeters)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2026-01-05
Last reviewed: 2026-04-02

- Salaries are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month.
- Double holiday pay (dubbel vakantiegeld) of 92% of the gross monthly salary is paid with the May salary.
- An end-of-year bonus equal to one monthly salary is paid in December.
- Salaries are indexed every January in line with the index for our joint committee.
- Meal vouchers are worth €8 per working day; the employee share is €1.09.
