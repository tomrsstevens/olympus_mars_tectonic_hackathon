COMPANY A FRANCE - ACCORD SUR LE TEMPS DE TRAVAIL / WORKING TIME AGREEMENT
Document owner: RH France (Julien Bernard)
Status: Approved

Last reviewed: 2026-02-11

The legal working week in France is 35 hours.
Company A France works a 37-hour schedule; the extra hours are compensated with RTT days.
Managers on a "forfait jours" contract work 218 days per year and do not count their hours.
