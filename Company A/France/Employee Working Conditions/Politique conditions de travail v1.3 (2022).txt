EMPLOYEE WORKING CONDITIONS POLICY
Company: Company A (France)
Policy ID: RH-FR-002
Effective date: 1 January 2022
Version: 1.3
Policy owner: RH France
Status: Superseded

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all permanent employees of Company A working under a French employment contract, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The standard full-time schedule consists of 39 hours per week. The extra hours above the legal 35 hours are compensated with 20 RTT days per year.

3. Annual Leave
Employees receive 25 working days of paid leave (congés payés) per year.

4. Remote Work
Télétravail is possible up to 1 day per week.

5. Sick Leave
Employees who are ill must send their sick note (arrêt de travail) within 48 hours.

6. Meal Vouchers
Employees receive titres-restaurant with a face value of €8 per working day.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Parental and special leave are granted in accordance with French employment law.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
