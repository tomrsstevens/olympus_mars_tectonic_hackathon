COMPANY A FRANCE - CONGÉS PAYÉS ET RTT / PAID LEAVE AND RTT
Document owner: RH France (Julien Bernard)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2024-06-01
Last reviewed: 2024-09-02

Paid leave: employees receive 25 working days of paid leave (five weeks) per year.
RTT: full-time employees on the 37-hour schedule receive 10 RTT days per year.
Main leave: take at least 12 consecutive days of leave between 1 May and 31 October.
