- Paid time off: 15 days a year for full-time employees, accrued each pay period; 20 days after five years of service. [1]
- Paid holidays: 10 a year, on top of PTO. [1]
- Carry-over: at most 5 unused days move to the next year and must be used by March 31;
  anything above that is lost on December 31. [1][2]
- US law does not require paid vacation; this is a company benefit. [1]
