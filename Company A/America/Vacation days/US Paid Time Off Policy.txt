COMPANY A - PAID TIME OFF (US)
Document owner: People Team (US)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2025-11-03
Last reviewed: 2026-02-20

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued each pay period.
After five years of service this increases to 20 days per year.

Carry-over: up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31.

Holidays: Company A also observes 10 paid holidays; see the holiday calendar in the portal.

Note: US federal law does not require paid vacation. This policy is a Company A benefit.
