Employee Working Conditions
Company A - America
Current approved policy — Effective 1 January 2026

Working hours
40 hours per week
  Monday to Friday, core hours 10:00 to 15:00
  Summer Fridays: offices close at 1 pm from Memorial Day to Labor Day
  Source: Employee Handbook - Working Conditions v4.0.txt; Email - Summer Fridays 2026.txt

Overtime
1.5 times the regular hourly rate
  For non-exempt employees, for hours over 40 in a workweek
  Must be approved in advance
  Source: Employee Handbook - Working Conditions v4.0.txt

Vacation
15 days of paid time off per year
  20 days after 5 years of service
  Up to 5 unused days carry over; the rest is lost on December 31
  Source: Employee Handbook - Working Conditions v4.0.txt; Email - PTO carry-over reminder.txt

Remote work
Up to 1 day per week
  From October 2026: 4 office days per week (announced 10 September 2026); handbook v4.0 says 2 remote days
  Source: Email - Office days from October.txt

Sick leave
7 paid sick days per year
  Doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days
  Since April 2026 (announced 1 April 2026); handbook v4.0 says 5 days
  Source: Email - More paid sick days.txt

Meal vouchers
Not offered in the United States
  Lunch is provided in the office on office days
  Source: Employee Handbook - Working Conditions v4.0.txt

Parental and special leave
12 weeks of paid parental leave
  Job protection under the FMLA where eligible
  Source: Employee Handbook - Working Conditions v4.0.txt
