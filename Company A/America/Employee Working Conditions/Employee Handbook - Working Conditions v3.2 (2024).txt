1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all regular employees of Company A in the United States, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The standard schedule is 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Non-exempt employees are paid overtime at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek.

3. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 12 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. Unused days cannot be carried over.

4. Remote Work
Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

5. Sick Leave
Employees receive 3 paid sick days per year.

6. Meal Vouchers
Company A does not offer meal vouchers in the United States.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Employees receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
