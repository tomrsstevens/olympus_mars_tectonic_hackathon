- Pay schedule: biweekly, every other Friday, by direct deposit; 26 paydays in 2026. [1][2]
- Payday on a bank holiday: you are paid the business day before. [2]
- Hourly staff: timesheets are due by noon on the Monday before each payday. [2]
- Pay errors: report them within 5 business days of payday; they are fixed in the next payroll. [1]
- W-2 forms: in the employee portal by January 31. [1]
