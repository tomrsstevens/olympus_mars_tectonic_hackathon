COMPANY A - US PAYROLL POLICY
Document owner: Payroll Operations (US)
Status: Approved
Last updated: 2026-01-12
Last reviewed: 2026-05-04
Applies to: all Company A employees in the United States

1. Pay frequency
Employees are paid biweekly, every other Friday, by direct deposit.

2. Minimum pay
The minimum wage at Company A is $48,000 per year for full-time employees in the United States.
Hourly employees are paid at least the applicable federal, state or local minimum wage, whichever is highest.

3. Payroll corrections
Report missing or incorrect pay to the payroll team within 5 business days of payday.
Corrections are paid in the next regular payroll.

4. Tax forms
W-2 forms are available in the employee portal by January 31 each year.
