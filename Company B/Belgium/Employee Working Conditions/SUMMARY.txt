Employee Working Conditions
Company B - Belgium
Current approved policy — Effective 1 September 2025

Working hours
40 hours per week
  12 ADV days per year bring the average back to 38 hours
  A four-day week is only a draft proposal and is not in force
  Source: Working Conditions Policy v1.2.txt

Overtime
150% of normal hourly rate
  200% on Sundays and public holidays
  Approve it in advance in the time-tracking tool
  Source: Working Conditions Policy v1.2.txt; Email - Overtime approval.txt

Vacation
20 days per year
  Plus 12 ADV days
  Collective closure 20 to 31 July 2026 uses 9 vacation days
  Source: Working Conditions Policy v1.2.txt; Email - Collective closure summer 2026.txt

Remote work
Up to 2 days per week
  Agreed with your manager
  Source: Working Conditions Policy v1.2.txt

Sick leave
Medical certificate required from the second day of illness
  No certificate for the first day, up to 3 times per year
  Since 1 February 2026 (announced 15 January 2026); policy v1.2 still says from the first day
  Source: Email - Sick leave without certificate.txt

Meal vouchers
€8 per working day
  Since 1 July 2026 (announced 1 June 2026); policy v1.2 says €7
  Source: Email - Meal vouchers go up in July.txt

Parental and special leave
Available according to Belgian employment law and company procedures.
  Source: Working Conditions Policy v1.2.txt
