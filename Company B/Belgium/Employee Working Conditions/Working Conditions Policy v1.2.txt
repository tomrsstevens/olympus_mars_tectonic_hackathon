1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all permanent employees of Company B working under a Belgian employment contract, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The full-time working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. To bring the average back to 38 hours per week, employees receive 12 ADV days per year. Approved overtime is paid at 150% of the standard hourly rate, and at 200% on Sundays and public holidays.

3. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 legal vacation days per calendar year, based on the days worked in the previous year. The 12 ADV days come on top of this.

4. Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager.

5. Sick Leave
Employees who are ill must inform their manager before the start of the working day. A medical certificate is required from the first day of illness.

6. Meal Vouchers
Employees receive meal vouchers worth €7 per working day actually worked.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Parental, birth, adoption and special leave are granted in accordance with Belgian employment legislation.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
