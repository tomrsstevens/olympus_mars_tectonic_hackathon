From: Sofie Maes, HR Belgium
To: All employees Belgium
Date: 2026-01-15
Subject: Sick leave: no certificate needed for the first day

Hi all,

From 1 February 2026, you no longer need a medical certificate for the first day of illness, up to 3 times per calendar year.
From the second day of illness, a medical certificate is still required.
Please keep informing your manager before the start of the working day.

Sofie
