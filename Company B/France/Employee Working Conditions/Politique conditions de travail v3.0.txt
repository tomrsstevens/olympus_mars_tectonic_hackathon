1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all permanent employees of Company B working under a French employment contract, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The working week is 35 hours. Hours above 35 per week are overtime: the first 8 overtime hours are paid at +25% and further hours at +50%. Overtime must be requested by the manager.

3. Annual Leave
Employees earn 2.5 days of paid leave (jours ouvrables) per month worked, i.e. 30 jours ouvrables or five weeks per year. The reference period runs from 1 June to 31 May.

4. Remote Work
Télétravail is possible up to 3 days per week, agreed with the employee's manager.

5. Sick Leave
Employees who are ill must send their sick note (arrêt de travail) to Human Resources within 48 hours.

6. Meal Vouchers
Employees receive titres-restaurant with a face value of €8.50 per working day. Company B pays 50% of the face value.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Parental, maternity, paternity and special leave are granted in accordance with French employment law. Company B keeps paying the full salary during the 25 days of paternity leave.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
