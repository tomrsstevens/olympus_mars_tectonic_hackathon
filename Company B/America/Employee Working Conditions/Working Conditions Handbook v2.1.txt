1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the general working conditions applicable to all regular employees of Company B in the United States, unless otherwise stated in an individual employment agreement, collective labour agreement or legally binding amendment. Where applicable legislation or an individual employment agreement provides more favourable conditions, those conditions shall take precedence.

2. Working Hours
The standard working time is 40 hours per week. Non-exempt employees are paid overtime at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek.

3. Annual Leave
Company B offers flexible paid time off (PTO) agreed with your manager. Employees are expected to take at least 10 days off per year, including one full week in a row. Company B also observes 11 paid holidays.

4. Remote Work
Employees in hybrid roles work from the office 3 days per week and may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

5. Sick Leave
Employees receive 5 paid sick days per year, or more where state law requires it. A doctor's note is required after 5 consecutive days of absence.

6. Meal Vouchers
Company B does not offer meal vouchers. Employees receive a $15 meal stipend for every day they work from the office.

7. Parental and Special Leave
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; other parents receive 8 weeks.

8. Policy Administration
Human Resources is responsible for the interpretation, implementation and maintenance of this policy. The company may amend this policy where required by changes in legislation, operational requirements or company practices. Any amendment must be formally approved and communicated to affected employees before becoming effective. The latest approved version supersedes all previous versions.
