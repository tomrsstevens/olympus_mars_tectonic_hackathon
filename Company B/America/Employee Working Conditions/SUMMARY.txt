Employee Working Conditions
Company B - America
Current approved policy — Effective 15 March 2025

Working hours
40 hours per week
  Source: Working Conditions Handbook v2.1.txt

Overtime
1.5 times the regular hourly rate
  For non-exempt employees, for hours over 40 in a workweek
  Source: Working Conditions Handbook v2.1.txt

Vacation
Flexible PTO, at least 10 days per year
  Including one full week in a row
  Plus 11 paid holidays in 2026
  A proposed minimum of 15 days is still a draft and not in force
  Source: Working Conditions Handbook v2.1.txt; Email - 2026 holiday calendar.txt

Remote work
Up to 1 day per week
  From October 2026: 4 office days, Monday to Thursday (announced 10 September 2026); handbook v2.1 says 2 remote days
  Source: Email - Office days from October.txt

Sick leave
5 paid sick days per year
  Or more where state law requires it
  Doctor's note required after 5 consecutive days
  Source: Working Conditions Handbook v2.1.txt

Meal vouchers
$15 meal stipend per office day
  No meal vouchers in the United States
  Source: Working Conditions Handbook v2.1.txt

Parental and special leave
16 weeks of paid parental leave for all parents
  Since July 2026 (announced 1 July 2026); handbook v2.1 gives other parents 8 weeks
  Source: Email - Parental leave for all parents.txt
